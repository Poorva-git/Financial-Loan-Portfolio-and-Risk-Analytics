# 📊 Business Insights

## Portfolio Overview

The loan portfolio consists of **38,576 loan applications**, with a total funded amount of **$435.76 million** and total repayments of **$473.07 million**. Since the total amount received exceeds the funded amount, the portfolio is generating positive returns through interest income and effective loan servicing.

The average loan amount is **$11,296**, while the average interest rate is **12%** and the average Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratio is **13%**, indicating that most borrowers maintain healthy financial profiles.

Among all loan applications:
- **83.33%** are Fully Paid
- **13.82%** are Charged Off
- **2.85%** are Current

This reflects a healthy lending portfolio with manageable credit risk.

---

## Loan Quality Analysis

Approximately **86.18%** of the portfolio consists of Good Loans (Fully Paid and Current), while **13.82%** are classified as Bad Loans (Charged Off).

Good loans received funding of **$370.22 million** and generated **$435.79 million** in repayments, demonstrating strong portfolio performance and interest income.

In contrast, Charged Off loans accounted for **$65.53 million** in funded loans but returned only **$37.28 million**, making them the primary source of credit losses.

---

## Monthly Lending Trends

Loan applications and funded amounts showed a consistent upward trend throughout **2021**, with lending activity peaking during the fourth quarter.

**December 2021** recorded:
- **4,314** loan applications
- **$53.98 million** funded

This indicates increased borrower demand and stronger lending activity toward the end of the year.

---

## Portfolio Segmentation

### State-wise Analysis

California recorded the highest lending activity with **6,894 loan applications**, followed by New York and Florida. These states represent the institution's largest lending markets and offer significant opportunities for future portfolio growth.

### Loan Purpose Analysis

**Debt Consolidation** was the most common borrowing purpose with **18,214 applications**, followed by Credit Card refinancing and Home Improvement loans. This indicates that borrowers primarily use loans to manage existing debt rather than finance new purchases.

### Home Ownership

Borrowers living in **rented properties** formed the largest customer segment, closely followed by mortgage holders. This suggests stronger lending demand among customers with ongoing housing-related financial obligations.

### Credit Grade Analysis

Grades **B, C, and D** accounted for the majority of charged-off loans, indicating higher default risk within these borrower groups. Credit grade proved to be one of the strongest indicators of loan performance.

---

## Risk Analysis

California experienced the highest number of charged-off loans, followed by Florida and New York. Since these states also have the highest lending volumes, geographic concentration risk should be monitored carefully.

Debt Consolidation and Small Business loans exhibited the highest default volumes, suggesting that borrowers applying for these loan purposes require more detailed credit assessment before loan approval.

Loan performance consistently declined from **Grade A** to **Grade G**. Grade A loans achieved a **94.30% Good Loan Rate**, while Grade G loans recorded only **68.69%**, confirming that the institution's credit grading system effectively differentiates borrower risk.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase monitoring of Debt Consolidation and Small Business loans due to higher default rates.
- Strengthen credit assessment for lower-grade borrowers (Grades D–G).
- Focus lending strategies on high-performing Grade A and Grade B customers.
- Monitor credit exposure in high-volume states such as California, Florida, and New York.
- Use borrower segmentation to deliver targeted loan products and personalized financial offers.
- Continue tracking monthly lending trends to optimize funding allocation and improve portfolio growth.
- Leverage dashboard insights to support data-driven lending decisions and long-term portfolio optimization.
